# Research and verification

Prioritize the fullest accessible original record and independent contemporary reporting. An appellate opinion is useful when one exists, but it is selective and may recount facts under a specific review standard. An indictment establishes allegations, testimony establishes what a witness said, and a judgment establishes the court's disposition. Check reversals, later findings and current status before narrating an outcome.

Authentic original posts or recorded interviews can establish what a person published or said, not necessarily the truth of every assertion. Documentary interviews may be valuable firsthand accounts; distinguish them from a filmmaker's reconstruction. Do not treat copies or syndications as independent confirmation.

Claim ledger: claim_id, statement, source_ids, precise locator, confidence, established/alleged/inference/unknown, conflicts, script segment. Timeline and ledger precede prose. Unsupported necessary details are cut, attributed or stated as gaps. Source hierarchy is contextual, not an automatic instruction to discard contradictions.

Inventory each source's title, author/agency, original URL, publication/update/access dates, type, locator, local path, download status, reuse rights, size and hash when a file was actually downloaded. Use downloaded_original, saved_excerpt, link_only, blocked or missing honestly. Self-written notes are not originals. Download appropriate public documents when useful; do not bypass access controls or collect harmful material for atmosphere.

State convictions with their dates and later relevant changes. Attribute allegations and disputed reconstructions. Avoid identifying private bystanders or minors unless necessary and justified for the actual story. Do not convert a facial expression, silence or diagnosis into evidence of guilt.

Check https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802008 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278 for relevant current rules. Distinguish removal/strikes, age restrictions and ad suitability. Context belongs in the video itself. Avoid graphic injury focus, sensational thumbnails and instructional wrongdoing. Muting a word or blurring an image is not a guarantee.

Track media rights individually. News clips and photographs are not automatically forbidden or automatically reusable; seek licensed, public-domain or otherwise justified uses and document uncertainty. Use clearly labelled original illustrations when rights are unresolved. Do not represent generated footage as real evidence.
